1 Disease Overview  
Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a chronic, relentlessly progressive interstitial lung disease in which repeated or dysregulated injury to the alveolar epithelium triggers aberrant repair, fibroblast proliferation and excess collagen deposition, leading to irreversible scarring of the pulmonary parenchyma and progressive loss of lung function. The disorder typically presents with slowly worsening exertional dyspnoea, dry “velcro-like” inspiratory crackles and digital clubbing, and radiology or histology that shows the usual interstitial pneumonia (UIP) pattern. IPF belongs to the broader class of fibrotic interstitial lung diseases (ILDs). 2 Synonyms & Abbreviations  
Historically the disease has been called “cryptogenic fibrosing alveolitis,” “idiopathic interstitial pneumonia–usual type,” or simply “fibrosing alveolitis,” and familial cases have been labelled “familial idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis” or “familial interstitial pneumonia.” The preferred modern abbreviation is IPF; UIP refers to the characteristic radiopathologic pattern rather than the disease itself. ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5450933/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

3 Subtypes / Variants  
No formal molecular subtypes are recognized, but clinically IPF is often stratified as sporadic versus familial (≈ 5–20 % of cases), as “rapid” versus “slow” functional progressors, and as “stable” disease versus episodes of acute exacerbation. Familial forms cluster in kindreds carrying rare telomerase or surfactant gene variants, whereas sporadic IPF usually lacks a clear monogenic cause. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39480160/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5824456/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

4 Epidemiology  
Population-based claims studies in the United States report prevalence of 14–64 cases per 100 000 using narrow versus broad case definitions, and incidence of 7–17 cases per 100 000 person-years (data 2010–2019). Among U.S. veterans the prevalence rose from 276 to 725 per 100 000 between 2010 and 2019 and incidence from 73 to 210 per 100 000 person-years. ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8867365/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [publications.ersnet.org](https://publications.ersnet.org/content/errev/21/126/355?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) IPF typically manifests after age 60 (median 67–75 y); < 5 % present before 50. Men are affected slightly more often, though recent administrative data show nearly equal sex distribution, and most cohorts are majority White. ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7337884/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10398946/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Because fewer than 200 000 Americans are estimated to live with IPF at any time, it meets the U.S. definition of a rare disease.  

5 Etiology & Pathogenesis  
IPF is idiopathic, but host susceptibility involves common polymorphisms—most strongly the MUC5B promoter variant rs35705950—and rare variants in telomerase (TERT, TERC), surfactant-related (SFTPC, SFTPA2) and other epithelial genes. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39480160/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Current concepts implicate repetitive epithelial micro-injury (e.g., to type II alveolar cells) followed by aberrant wound healing driven by cytokines such as TGF-β, PDGF, and Wnt mediators, activation of fibroblastic foci and extracellular-matrix accumulation. ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5824456/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20952439/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Established environmental or lifestyle risk factors include cigarette smoking, occupational exposures to metal/wood/stone dusts or livestock, chronic gastro-oesophageal reflux with micro-aspiration, and possibly viral infections and air pollution. ([pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6777743/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [publications.ersnet.org](https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erj/53/2/1801699?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) No inheritance pattern is seen in sporadic IPF; familial forms show autosomal-dominant transmission with variable penetrance.  

6 Clinical Presentation  
Early features usually include progressive exertional dyspnoea and a persistent dry cough; fine “Velcro” basal crackles on auscultation are almost universal. Digital clubbing, bibasilar inspiratory squeaks, weight loss, fatigue and reduced exercise tolerance develop over time. Late manifestations include resting hypoxaemia, cyanosis, right-sided heart failure secondary to pulmonary hypertension and severe functional impairment. Acute exacerbations present with sudden worsening dyspnoea, new bilateral ground-glass infiltrates and high mortality. Presentation varies: some patients decline steadily, others have stepwise drops punctuated by exacerbations, and about 10–20 % show rapid loss of forced vital capacity (FVC) within 6–12 months. Major complications encompass acute exacerbation, progressive respiratory failure, secondary pulmonary hypertension, lung cancer (≈ 5–22 % lifetime risk) and infection. ([mdpi.com](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3271/6/3/71?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [onlinelibrary.wiley.com](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/crj.12631?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7337884/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

7 Diagnostic Evaluation  
Diagnosis requires a compatible clinical scenario and exclusion of alternative ILDs, ideally via multidisciplinary discussion. Bedside clues are bibasilar crackles, clubbing and impaired diffusing capacity. Serum tests include complete blood count, autoantibody panel and biomarkers such as KL-6; none are diagnostic but help exclude mimics. High-resolution CT is central: a definite UIP pattern—subpleural, basal-predominant reticulation with honeycombing—confirms IPF without biopsy, while “probable UIP” may suffice per 2022 ATS/ERS/JRS/ALAT guidelines if all other data fit. ([radiopaedia.org](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/diagnostic-hrct-criteria-for-usual-interstitial-pneumonia-uip-pattern-atsersjrsalat-2018/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [thelancet.com](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanres/article/PIIS2213-2600%2822%2900223-5/abstract?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) If imaging is indeterminate, histologic UIP can be obtained by surgical lung biopsy or transbronchial cryobiopsy in experienced centres. Genetic testing is considered in patients < 50 y or with a family history, using telomere- and surfactant-gene panels. Formal diagnostic guidance is provided by the 2018 and updated 2022 ATS/ERS/JRS/ALAT statements. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35486072/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

8 Management & Standard Therapy  
Current first-line pharmacotherapy consists of the antifibrotic agents nintedanib 150 mg orally twice daily or pirfenidone titrated to 2 403 mg/day in three divided doses; both slow annual FVC decline by ≈ 50 %. ([drugs.com](https://www.drugs.com/monograph/nintedanib.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [fda.report](https://fda.report/DailyMed/cbbd14a4-b7e6-4147-9314-bb715dfb1a51?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) For advanced disease, bilateral lung transplantation remains the only intervention that can restore lung function; candidates are referred early owing to rapid progression. When disease worsens despite antifibrotics, switching drugs, combining with clinical-trial agents or enrolment in hospice/palliative care is recommended. Supportive measures include long-term oxygen therapy, pulmonary rehabilitation programs that improve 6-minute-walk distance and quality of life, vaccination against influenza, pneumococcus and COVID-19, smoking cessation, weight optimisation and management of comorbidities such as GERD (proton-pump inhibitors) or pulmonary hypertension. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34353031/?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [publications.ersnet.org](https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erj/53/2/1801699?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

9 Investigational / Emerging Therapies  
Several novel agents are under active clinical study. The oral selective phosphodiesterase-4B inhibitor BI 1015550 (nerandomilast) met its Phase 3 FIBRONEER-IPF primary endpoint in 2024–2025 by further slowing FVC loss on top of standard antifibrotic therapy. ([ajmc.com](https://www.ajmc.com/view/nerandomilast-slows-lung-function-decline-in-patients-with-ipf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Pamrevlumab (anti-CTGF antibody) failed to meet primary efficacy in the Phase 3 ZEPHYRUS-1 trial, leading to study discontinuation, but mechanistic insights persist. ([investor.fibrogen.com](https://investor.fibrogen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fibrogen-announces-topline-results-phase-3-zephyrus-1-study?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Zinpentraxin-α (recombinant human pentraxin-2) was terminated for futility in the Phase 3 STARSCAPE study. ([atsjournals.org](https://www.atsjournals.org/doi/10.1164/rccm.202401-0116OC?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Other programmes include integrin αvβ6 blockers (bexotegrast/PLN-74809), autotaxin inhibitors, anti-LOXL2 antibodies and mesenchymal stem-cell or telomerase-modifying approaches; trials can be searched on ClinicalTrials.gov under the term “Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis.”  

10 Prognosis  
Untreated IPF carries a median life expectancy of three to five years from diagnosis, with 5-year survival ≈ 30–45 %. ([verywellhealth.com](https://www.verywellhealth.com/pulmonary-fibrosis-types-symptoms-diagnosis-6891013?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9488716/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Antifibrotic therapy modestly improves survival: pooled analyses show 3-year cumulative survival rising above 60 % in the post-2010 era when nintedanib and pirfenidone became available. ([publications.ersnet.org](https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erjor/8/1/00591-2021?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Predictors of poorer outcome include older age, male sex, low baseline FVC or DLCO, rapid FVC decline (> 10 %/yr), pulmonary hypertension, acute exacerbation, and higher GAP stage. ([respiratory-research.biomedcentral.com](https://respiratory-research.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12931-016-0454-0?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [onlinelibrary.wiley.com](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/crj.12631?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) Early referral for transplantation, enrollment in clinical trials and comprehensive supportive care can favorably modify the otherwise bleak natural history.
